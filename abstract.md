Let $G$ be a finite simple graph with $m$ edges and degrees
$d_1 \le d_2 \le \dots \le d_n$ in nondecreasing order. The annihilation number
$a(G)$ is the largest $k$ such that $\sum_{i=1}^{k} d_i \le m$. We formalize
the bound $\alpha(G) \le a(G)$ on the independence number, due to Pepper. The
proof observes that the vertex degrees of an independent set sum to at most
$m$, since every edge has at most one endpoint in the set, and that the $k$
smallest degrees sum to at most the degrees of any $k$ vertices.
